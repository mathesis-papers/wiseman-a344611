Strictness and sibling corrections for OEIS A344611, FORMULA, with an alternative derivation of Manyama's (2026) convolution

Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

Published paper · Version 2 · CC BY 4.0

PDFSourceMathesis recordLean source

Abstract

For OEIS A344611, FORMULA (revision 27), we prove A344611(n) <= A160786(n) for every n >= 0 and give an alternative derivation of Manyama's (2026) convolution, complementing Andrews–El Bachraoui's (2026) strict-partition formulas. An injection sends a partition with nonnegative reverse-alternating sum to an odd-length partition of the next weight. We characterize equality, give an explicit lower bound for the deficit, and deduce monotonicity of the corresponding unrestricted count. The same parity analysis proves a shifted companion comparison, determines all plateaus of the negative-sum count, and corrects neighboring indexing and distinct-part descriptions. A bivariate series settles the linked ordered-composition interpretations, with their precise shifts.
